the tail

the overwhelming consensus is it looks like a robot dildo. if by chance you work at lovense or bad dragon, hmu.

the reason it looks like a zased and xhristpilled femboy twitch streamer's funkopop is because it's modeled after a spine, the rubber bands act as tendons that oppose gravity and keep the segments (or vertebrae) perpendicular to the user's body. this lowers how much work the servos need to do significantly, all they gotta do is oppose the rubber bands.

i would love to add some insane mathematical equations here showing how the mechanics and physics of this works, truth is i didn't calculate shit. this was all done by trying different rubber bands and servos until i found a good combo.

servos

these loud whiny moany little guys move the tail by pulling a string that runs through each vertebrae segment, eventually being attached at the tip.

you'd think we're working in 3d here, but the nature of [i forgot the robotics term for it] kinematics means it's just two dimensions, as the tail will curve inwards towards the user as it's pulled to one direction. the code only really has to translate x-y coordinates to degrees for each servo.
